File: photo.webp (1688 x 3008), used by styles.css on the official portfolio.
Browser image URL: https://newgentle.github.io/photo.webp
The WebAR card and profile load the ORIGINAL portrait from this URL.

If this photo is renamed on the portfolio, update img src in index.html, ar.html, ar-hd.html and design-preview.html.
For full offline deployment, download `photo.webp` from the repository and change those src attributes to `assets/photo.webp`.
